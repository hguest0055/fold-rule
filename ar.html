AR measurement reads geometry from motion and depth, not from a calibrated scale. Expect roughly 1–3% error on a metre, worse on dark, glossy or featureless surfaces, and poor results under about 5 cm. For anything small, the on-glass ruler is the accurate tool — this is for things too big to lay on the phone.

Point the reticle at a surface, then tap to drop a point.
